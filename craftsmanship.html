FLOW CREATIVE / CRAFTSMANSHIP

From Raw Material to Finished Craft.

A visual storytelling sequence following the broad stages behind a finished piece — from material selection through final inspection.

01 — Material SelectionFLOW / PROCESS

Choosing the Starting Material

Appropriate steel and handle materials are considered for the intended object and its visual language.

02 — ForgingFLOW / PROCESS

Traditional Metalworking

Heat, pressure and shaping establish the broad form of the metalwork.

03 — Heat TreatmentFLOW / PROCESS

Developing Steel Characteristics

Controlled thermal treatment is part of how steel reaches its intended material characteristics.

04 — Grinding & ShapingFLOW / PROCESS

Refining Form

Surfaces and proportions are refined until the piece begins to reveal its final silhouette.

05 — PolishingFLOW / PROCESS

Bringing Out the Finish

Progressive finishing reveals reflections, texture and subtle surface transitions.

06 — EtchingFLOW / PROCESS

Revealing the Pattern

Surface treatment can increase contrast between steel layers and make the Damascus pattern visible.

07 — Handle CraftsmanshipFLOW / PROCESS

Fitting the Human Element

Wood, composite or natural materials are shaped and finished as part of the whole object.

08 — Final InspectionFLOW / PROCESS

The Finished Object

The completed piece is checked for visual consistency, finish quality and overall presentation.

The philosophy
The finished object is only as convincing as the story visible in its surfaces.
